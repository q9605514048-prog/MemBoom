4. Local storage and remembered entry
Local vault contents are encrypted on your device. On the website, “Remember sign-in on this device” stores an encrypted unlock secret and a non-exportable browser key in IndexedDB; it may also remember a server session. This does not protect against malware or hostile scripts running in the same origin. Use this option only on a device you trust. “Lock” clears remembered entry; uncheck the option to use password-based entry again. Private browsing, browser restrictions, or clearing storage can prevent remembered entry.
